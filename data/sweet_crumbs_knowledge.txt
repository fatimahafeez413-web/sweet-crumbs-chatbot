# Sweet Crumbs Bakery - Customer Information
(Sweet Crumbs is a fictional bakery made up for this portfolio project.)

## About Sweet Crumbs
Sweet Crumbs is a small family-run bakery in the town of Brightwater. It was founded in 2016 by Maya and Daniel Ortiz. Everything is baked fresh every morning using local flour and real butter.

## Location and Contact
Address: 42 Maple Street, Brightwater.
Phone: (555) 013-2244.
Email: hello@sweetcrumbs.example
Instagram: @sweetcrumbs.bakery
Parking: free 2-hour street parking on Maple Street, plus a small lot behind the shop. The front entrance is wheelchair accessible.

## Opening Hours
Monday to Friday: 7:00am to 6:00pm.
Saturday: 8:00am to 5:00pm.
Sunday: 8:00am to 1:00pm.
We are closed on Christmas Day, New Year's Day and Thanksgiving. On Christmas Eve we close at 2:00pm.
Popular items often sell out, so on weekends it is best to come before 10:00am.

## Menu: Cakes, Cookies and Brownies
Cake slice: $5.50. Flavours are chocolate fudge, carrot with cream cheese frosting, lemon drizzle and red velvet.
Ready-made whole 8-inch cake: $38, serves about 8. Only chocolate fudge and carrot are available as ready-made whole cakes.
Cookies: $2.50 each, or a box of 6 for $13. Flavours are chocolate chip, oatmeal raisin, double chocolate and peanut butter.
Brownie: $3.75.

## Menu: Drinks

## Custom Cakes
Custom cakes must be ordered at least 5 days ahead.
Starting prices: 6-inch (serves 6 to 8) from $45; 8-inch (serves 10 to 12) from $65; 10-inch (serves 16 to 20) from $90.
Writing on the cake costs $5 extra. Fondant decorations cost from $15 extra.
A 50% deposit is paid when you order, and the rest is paid at pickup.
Design changes are free up to 3 days before pickup.
If you cancel at least 72 hours before pickup, the deposit is refunded. If you cancel later, the deposit is not refunded.

## Delivery and Pickup
We deliver within 5 miles of the shop, Tuesday to Saturday only, between 10:00am and 4:00pm. We do not deliver on Sunday or Monday.
Delivery fee: $6 for orders under $50. Delivery is free for orders of $50 or more.
Custom cake delivery costs $12, whatever the order total.
Delivery orders must be placed by 2:00pm the day before.
Pickup is free. You can order online or by phone, and regular items are ready in about 30 minutes.

## Allergies and Dietary Options
Our kitchen handles wheat, milk, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts and soy. Because of this, we cannot guarantee that any product is completely free of allergens.
Nuts: almond croissants and peanut butter cookies contain nuts. No item is guaranteed nut-free.
Vegan options: sourdough loaf, baguette and the double chocolate cookie.
Gluten-free options: flourless chocolate cake slice (Fridays and Saturdays only) and almond macarons.
A full ingredient list is available on request at the counter.

## Catering and Large Orders
Orders of more than 30 items need 48 hours notice.
Breakfast box: $60, serves 10 people.
Cookie platter: 24 cookies for $55.
Businesses get a 10% discount on orders over $200 and can request an invoice.

## Payment and Refunds
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept checks.
Gift cards are available in $10 steps.
If there is a problem with your order, tell us within 24 hours and send a photo. We will replace it or refund you.
Fresh items cannot be returned if you simply change your mind.

## Storage and Freshness
Bread is best within 2 days. Sliced bread can be frozen for up to 1 month.
Croissants are best on the day they are baked. Reheat for 5 minutes at 175 C (350 F).
Cakes with cream cheese frosting or fresh cream must be kept in the fridge and eaten within 3 days.
Cookies stay fresh for 5 days in an airtight container.

## Loyalty Program
The Sweet Crumbs Club is free to join, at the counter or online.
You earn 1 point for every $1 you spend. 100 points give you $5 credit.
You get a free cookie during your birthday month.
Points expire after 12 months.

## Classes, Seating and Other Information
Baking classes run on the first Saturday of every month, from 10:00am to 12:00pm. The price is $45 per person and there is a maximum of 12 people. Sign up at the counter.
Seating: 16 seats inside and 8 seats on the patio. Free Wi-Fi is available.
Dogs are allowed on the patio but not inside the shop. Assistance dogs are welcome inside.
We sometimes hire baker assistants. Send your CV to jobs@sweetcrumbs.example.
